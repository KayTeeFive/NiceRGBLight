# 🔌 Wiring / Connection (Arduino Pro Mini)

- Connect LED strip data pins to `DATA_PIN_1` and `DATA_PIN_2`
- Connect LED strip VCC to 5V supply
- Connect LED strip GND to common ground with Arduino Pro Mini
- Connect button between `BUTTON_PIN` and GND

> ⚠️ Use an external 5V power supply for LED strips longer than ~10 LEDs.
Always share a common ground between Arduino Pro Mini and LED power supply.
